Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no ads, and analytics only if you allow them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which projects and searches people use. It only loads after you choose Allow, and it then sets Google Analytics cookies. If you choose No thanks, nothing is loaded and nothing is sent. We don't use the data for advertising.

Stored in your browser

Your light or dark theme and your analytics choice are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

Other requests

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts.
  • The star count on the GitHub link is fetched from the GitHub API.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.

AI Rebuilds links to other people's projects and hosts no software. Each project belongs to its authors.
