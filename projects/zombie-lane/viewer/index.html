Asset viewer

Open your own copy of the Zombie Lane APK to browse and play its 18,670 animations and see its starting scenes. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

      –
      Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

      How it works

      The viewer unzips the APK in memory, reads the animation table assets/r1 and the texture bundles, and draws each frame with Canvas 2D. Names come from the game's own resource list in classes.dex. The formats are documented in sprites-and-animations.md, and the code in lib/ will be reused by the rebuild.

      Scenes places the game's own layout tables on the isometric grid: the home a new player starts with, and Rob's place. Placement, projection, draw order and each object's idle animation follow scenes-and-saves.md.

      Not yet supported: particle emitters (6 frames in the whole game), timeline color tints (only transparency is applied), the player's avatar in scenes, and the game's draw deferral for objects that overlap several tiles.

      Community-run. Analysis powered by REA. No original game files are hosted here.
