Zombie Lane

Digital Chocolate's zombie-survival builder game. The Android build depended on Facebook login and online services from 2015.

    About the original

    Zombie Lane was a social survival game by Digital Chocolate. It first ran on Facebook, and an Android version followed. You built up your neighborhood while fighting off zombies. Like many social games of its time, it relied on Facebook login and online purchases, so it stopped working when those services shut down.

    Rights status: unknown. Needs research before any assets are reused in a public build.

    Rebuild plan

    1. Analysis: map the game loop, save format, building and zombie rules, and asset archive layout.
    2. Offline rules: swap every online dependency (login, purchases, friends, offers) for local state.
    3. Rebuild: write a Canvas/TypeScript version from the specs. Players load the original art from their own APK in the browser. Nothing is uploaded or hosted here.
